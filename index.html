Screenshot Privacy · 1.0.0 · Free & open source

Open. Cover. Review.

  1. Open, drop, or paste one still PNG, JPG, or WebP screenshot.
  2. Choose Cover and drag a rectangle over private details. Use Select to move it or drag its handles to resize.
  3. Choose Crop to keep only part of the screenshot, then click Apply crop.
  4. Click Review & export. Inspect the actual exported PNG, then save it or copy it where supported.

How the covers work

Every cover is a fully opaque black or white rectangle. The exported PNG is newly rendered: pixels inside those rectangles are painted over. It contains no editable layers or undo history. The export removes optional PNG metadata chunks and uses a generic filename.

This is manual redaction. The app does not find personal information automatically. Details outside your covers remain visible. Inspect the entire exported image, including small text and the edges of rectangles, before sharing it.

Local, temporary, and under your control

The app makes no network requests and does not store screenshots in an app history, browser database, or account. Work stays available in the open tab. Closing or reloading clears your editing session. Export a finished PNG to keep it. The original file on your computer is not changed.

A finished export is a separate image. Share that exported PNG, not your original screenshot or a screen capture of the editor.

Exact covers and keyboard access

Use Add cover to place a rectangle without dragging. Select it in the Covers list and enter its X, Y, width, and height, then click Apply size. Extra coverage adds up to 16 pixels around the cover; the default is 2. All output rectangle edges land on whole image pixels.

Image support

Still PNG, JPG, and WebP inputs up to 30 MB, 20 megapixels, and 16,000 px on either side. Animated images, PDFs, GIF, HEIC, and SVG are not supported. One image at a time; up to 100 covers and 60 undo steps. Output is PNG at the crop’s pixel size, with transparent areas flattened onto white.

Open index.html in a modern desktop browser with JavaScript enabled. Image clipboard copying depends on browser support and permissions; Save PNG is the fallback. Ctrl/⌘ V pastes an image when your clipboard contains one. There is no project save or automatic recovery after a reload.
